FAQ

Everything you need to know

What is a Smart Swap?

A Smart Swap is a value exchange: you offer fans something they want (early access to music, exclusive content, behind-the-scenes footage) and in return, they share their email, phone number, and pre-save your release — all in one step. Average opt-in rate is 92%.

How is this different from tools like Laylo or Linktree?

Link-in-bio tools like Linktree send fans away with zero data capture. Laylo captures data, but only as isolated drop alerts — there is no community layer and no compounding across releases. Smart Swaps capture fan contact information, build a direct relationship, and compound your audience with every release you put out.

What data do Smart Swaps capture?

Email addresses, phone numbers, pre-saves (Spotify, Apple Music), Spotify follows, YouTube subscriptions, and fan location data. All captured in a single step with 92% average opt-in.

Do fans actually opt in?

Yes. Smart Swaps have a 92% average opt-in rate because fans receive genuine value — early access to music they want. This is not a generic email gate. It is a value exchange that fans are excited to complete.

Can I use Smart Swaps for things other than music releases?

Yes. Artists use Smart Swaps for tour announcements, merch drops, exclusive content, fan voting, behind-the-scenes access, and more. Any moment where you can offer exclusive value works.

What happens to the data I capture?

All fan data is yours. It syncs to your Grouped dashboard where you can view fan profiles, segment audiences, and reach them directly via email or push notifications. You can also export data to your existing CRM tools.

How much does it cost?

Smart Swaps are available on all Grouped plans, including the free tier. Free accounts can create up to 3 Smart Swaps. Paid plans (starting at $7/month) unlock unlimited swaps, custom branding, and advanced analytics.

How do I get started?

Sign up for a free Grouped account, create your first Smart Swap in under 2 minutes, and share the link anywhere — bio, stories, ads, email. Fans tap, swap, and you own the relationship.
