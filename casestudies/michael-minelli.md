# Michael Minelli Creates & Sells Thousands in Merch from a Fan-Designed Shirt

**Zero upfront cost. Zero inventory. Zero risk. All reward.**

---

## The Setup

Michael Minelli cracked the code on how to engage his fans, generate new revenue, and create viral momentum — all with virtually zero upfront time, cost, or risk.

He launched a fan-driven T-shirt design competition inside his Grouped community. The goal: create an official "Minelli Family" shirt — something fans could wear to shows and instantly recognize other Group members in real life. This wasn't just merch. It was a badge of belonging.

---

## How the Campaign Ran

**Step 1 — Design Submissions via Discussion Forums**
Fans submitted their T-shirt designs through the Discussion Tab inside his Group.

**Step 2 — Livestream Review**
Minelli hosted a Zoom-like livestream inside Grouped where fans could join on camera and present their designs directly to him and the community. It became an interactive, co-creative event — not just a reveal.

**Step 3 — Image Poll Vote**
He narrowed the submissions to the top five, then posted an image poll inside the Group. Fans voted on the winner. This step also served as pre-sales validation: fans who voted were highly likely to purchase the design they backed.

**Step 4 — Print on Demand Fulfillment**
The winning design was made available via Print on Demand. No upfront costs. No inventory. No logistical headaches. The fan behind the winning design received a lifetime membership, a free shirt, and permanent bragging rights.

---

## The Result

- Thousands of dollars in merch revenue
- A full month of organic content (submissions, livestream, voting, announcement)
- Deep fan engagement with zero artist effort after the initial prompt
- Pre-validated product — fans voted for what they already wanted to buy

---

## How It Worked

The Grouped platform served as the creative studio, focus group, and storefront all in one campaign. Fans did the creative work. The image poll validated demand before production. The Discussion Forum and Livestream tools kept the experience inside the community, not scattered across DMs and external platforms.

---

## Key Takeaway

Your Group is a creative studio, focus group, and storefront. The fans did the work, validated the product before it launched, and bought it because they owned the outcome. Minelli spent almost nothing — and earned meaningful revenue, content, and loyalty in return.
