# MIKE. Gets New Paid Subscribers While He Sleeps Using Instagram Automations

**How MIKE. and Kilmer turned Instagram comments into frictionless subscriber conversion — automatically.**

---

## The Setup

MIKE. and his manager Kilmer found a simple but effective way to turn existing Instagram engagement into paid Group subscribers — without spending extra time or making it feel like a sales pitch.

Using **ManyChat** and **GetPinPoint** — third-party tools that automate Instagram DMs and comment responses based on keywords — they created a system that ran itself.

---

## How It Worked

1. MIKE. posts on Instagram and prompts fans to comment a specific keyword
2. Any fan who comments that keyword **automatically receives a DM** with a link to join OnlySteves
3. The link has a **free trial code pre-applied** — fans land on a signup page with the trial already unlocked

The result: fans don't feel like they're being sold a subscription. They feel like they've **unlocked something exclusive** — like they "won" access that not everyone gets. That emotional shift made all the difference in conversion.

---

## Why It Worked

The mechanism flipped the psychology of subscription sales. A standard "join my $9/month community" ask can feel transactional. A DM that says "here's your free access code since you engaged with my post" feels like a reward.

Because the automation ran through Grouped's free trial system, every new signup started with a full trial period — lowering the barrier to entry and giving fans time to experience the community before they were ever asked to pay.

---

## Key Takeaway

Grouped doesn't require you to build a new audience. MIKE. used the Instagram audience he already had — and built a system that converts that engagement into owned subscribers automatically. The community grows while the artist sleeps.

**Note:** ManyChat and GetPinPoint are third-party tools, not native Grouped features. This workflow is fully supported but requires a separate account with one of those platforms. Grouped handles the free trial mechanics and community management on its end.
