# PardyAlone Turns Tour M&G Tickets Into $35,000+ Annual Recurring Revenue

**From one-time upsell to lifetime fan support — all through community.**

---

## The Setup

Instead of following the traditional artist playbook of charging fans $20–$100 for a one-time Meet & Greet, PardyAlone took a bold, fan-first approach: he didn't sell Meet & Greet tickets at all.

The only way to meet PardyAlone after a show was by joining his exclusive community tier — the Private Pardy — for just **$5/month**. This small monthly commitment gave fans direct access and exclusive experiences that far outweighed the cost.

Fans signed up on the spot at the merch booth just to get a chance to meet him.

---

## The Numbers

| Model | Revenue |
|---|---|
| Traditional M&G (one-time) | ~$22,000 |
| Grouped recurring model | $35,000+ annually |

- **~700 fans** signed up at $5/month
- **82% retention** after 6 months — 590 fans still subscribed
- On track for **$35,000+ annually**, potentially for years, even without touring again

---

## Quote

> "Instead of charging fans an upsell of $20–$100 for getting the chance to meet PardyAlone, he decided he wouldn't sell Meet & Greet tickets at all — instead, he used his paid tier as the only way to meet him on tour."

---

## How It Worked

PardyAlone reframed the M&G not as a transaction but as a membership perk. Fans at shows were told: "The only way to meet me is to be in the community." For $5/month, it was an easy yes — especially compared to the $20–$100 they'd have paid for a one-time experience.

The offer was compelling enough that fans signed up at the merch booth mid-tour, turning every show date into a subscriber acquisition moment.

---

## Key Takeaway

One-time transactions leave money and relationships on the table. Converting a tour upsell into a recurring membership compounded both revenue and fan loyalty. PardyAlone didn't give up the M&G — he made it more valuable by making it exclusive to members.
