# SoMo Sells Out a Private Album Release Party + Adds Livestream Tickets

**How SoMo turned his Grouped community into a sold-out event venue — and livestreamed it for the fans who couldn't make it.**

---

## The Setup

When SoMo prepared to debut his latest album, *9 Lives*, he didn't go the traditional route. Instead, he turned to his fan community on TheFanmily.com — built and powered by Grouped — to host an exclusive release party accessible only through membership.

Attendance wasn't open to the public. Access was structured entirely around his Grouped community tiers.

---

## The Event Structure

**Premium Tier ($35/month) — 30+ super-supporters, $1,000+ LTV each**
Members in this tier received a free in-person ticket as a thank-you for their continued support, plus early access to discounted companion tickets at 50% off.

**General Group Members**
All other Group members could purchase an in-person ticket for $100.

**Remote Fans**
Fans who couldn't attend physically could purchase a $20 livestream ticket. The livestream featured two camera angles and was hosted directly within the Grouped platform — no third-party tool required.

---

## The Result

- **Sold out the event**
- **Thousands of dollars in ticket revenue**
- Rewarded loyalty in his most dedicated tier
- Extended the event to remote fans via livestream
- Deepened connection with his biggest supporters

## How It Worked

SoMo used Grouped as the single infrastructure layer for an event that had three different audience segments — VIPs, general community members, and remote fans. Each segment had a different price point and access level, all managed inside the platform.

The livestream component is particularly notable: instead of relying on YouTube Live or a third-party streaming tool, the event lived inside Grouped, keeping fans in the community context where they already had a relationship with SoMo.

---

## Key Takeaway

Premiere access isn't just for music. SoMo used it for a live event — and structured it so that the community itself was the ticket. Fans who were already members were rewarded. Fans who weren't had a compelling reason to join before the next one.
