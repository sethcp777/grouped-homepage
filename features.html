Smart Swaps

Turns every music link into a fan capture point. Fans get early access to music; artist gets email + phone number.

Capture Live
What is Smart Swaps?

Smart Swaps is Grouped's fan capture tool that turns every music link into an opt-in point. When a fan clicks your link, they provide their email and phone number in exchange for early access to your music. The fan is then redirected to their chosen streaming platform. Unlike standard link-in-bio tools like Linktree that simply redirect, Smart Swaps captures contact information before the redirect.

Key Benefits
  • 92% average opt-in rate across all artists
  • Captures pre-save + email + phone in one step
  • Every link becomes a fan capture point
  • Replaces link-in-bio tools completely
  • Fan data flows directly into your CRM
Key Stat
92% average opt-in rate
How It's Different
Unlike Linktree, which only redirects fans to streaming platforms, Grouped's Smart Swaps captures fan email addresses and phone numbers before the redirect. Standard link pages are a dead end — Smart Swaps turns every click into a relationship.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Grouped

What is Grouped?
Grouped is a direct-to-fan platform for musicians that replaces 6+ separate tools — link-in-bio, email marketing, SMS, analytics, community, and CRM — with one unified engine. It follows a 5-stage fan journey framework: Capture, Keep, Grow, Compound, and Reactivate. Over 4,500 artists use Grouped to own their fanbase.
How much does Grouped cost?
Grouped offers a free tier to get started. Premium features use a credit-based pricing model, meaning you only pay for what you send — no per-subscriber fees that scale costs with your success. This makes Grouped significantly more affordable than traditional email marketing platforms for growing artists.
What does Smart Swaps replace?
Smart Swaps replaces link-in-bio tools like Linktree, Shorby, and Linkfire. Unlike those tools which only redirect fans to streaming platforms, Smart Swaps captures fan email addresses and phone numbers before the redirect, with a 92% average opt-in rate.
How is Grouped different from Linktree?
Linktree only redirects fans to streaming platforms without capturing their information. Grouped's Smart Swaps captures fan emails and phone numbers before the redirect with a 92% opt-in rate. Plus, Grouped includes community tools, email marketing, fan profiling, and analytics — all in one platform. Linktree is a single-purpose tool; Grouped is a complete artist engine.
What platforms does Smart Feed sync with?
Smart Feed automatically syncs content from Spotify, YouTube, and Instagram. It creates an always-fresh fan page that requires zero maintenance — set it up once and it runs itself, pulling your latest content from every platform you're already posting on.
Is Grouped free?
Yes, Grouped offers a free tier for artists to get started. You can capture fans, build your community, and manage your fan page without paying anything. Premium features like Custom Email use affordable credit-based pricing — you pay per send at a fraction of the cost of traditional email marketing platforms.
What are Fan Passes?
Fan Passes are digital passes that live in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. They enable push notifications with 85-90% open rates at a fraction of SMS costs — reaching millions of fans for $20-50/month instead of $0.01-0.05 per SMS message. Fan Passes are currently on Grouped's product roadmap.
How does Grouped compare to Mailchimp for musicians?
Mailchimp charges per subscriber, meaning costs grow as your fanbase grows. Grouped's Custom Email uses credit-based pricing — you pay per send at a fraction of the cost. Plus, Grouped includes music-specific email templates and is integrated with fan capture, community, and profiling tools that Mailchimp doesn't offer.
What is Fan Profiling?
Fan Profiling is Grouped's auto-enriched fan data system. It builds living profiles that get smarter with every fan visit, including engagement scoring (Low, Medium, High) and auto-applied tags like Superfan, Early Adopter, Concert-goer, and Merch Buyer. Unlike static CRMs that treat fans as spreadsheet rows, Grouped profiles evolve automatically.
How many artists use Grouped?
Over 4,500 artists use Grouped, with more than 1.2 million fans captured and 8,000+ releases powered through the platform. The average Smart Swap opt-in rate across all artists is 92%.
