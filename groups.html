FAQ

Everything you need to know

What are Groups?

Your Group is a customizable, artist-owned fan space. It gives you a place to keep fans close to your music, your updates, and the biggest moments in your career.

How are Groups different from Discord?

Groups are built around the artist-fan relationship, not around running a separate server. They give you a simpler, more focused place to keep fans close without all the noise.

Do I have to constantly manage it?

No. Groups are meant to be simpler and lower-lift than traditional community tools, so you can stay connected with fans without taking on another full-time job.

How do fans join my Group?

You can invite fans directly through your invite page, import a CSV of your email list and send invitations to your existing fans, or launch Swaps that capture fan data and funnel those fans into your Group.

What can I share in my Group?

You can share music, videos, updates, release moments, and the things you want fans to see first.

Are Groups free?

Yes. Launching a Group is free for every artist using Grouped. Paid tiers unlock more advanced features, including monetization tools.

Who are Groups for?

Groups are for artists who want a clean, organized, music-first space to bring fans together under one roof, reach them directly, share media, monetize, and own the relationship and data tied to their audience.

Can Groups grow with me over time?

Yes. Groups are designed to grow with your audience without punishing you for growth the way traditional CRMs often do. They give you a more scalable and cost-effective way to stay connected with fans as your career grows.

Why would I use Groups instead of just social media?

Social media is great for discovery, but it is not built for ownership. Groups gives you a place to bring fans together, reach them directly, and keep the relationship going in a space that is actually yours.

Why launch a Group now?

It’s never too soon to start owning your fan relationships and taking back control of your fan data. Every release is a chance to pull more fans off rented channels and into a space you own, so your growth compounds instead of resets.
