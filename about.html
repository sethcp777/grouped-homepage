About Grouped

We built a platform where artists matter and fans belong.

Grouped exists because the relationship between artists and fans deserves better than an algorithm. We're here to make sure it gets it.

Mission

Music first, always.

Grouped exists because artists deserve control over their moments. We give them the tools to share music on their own terms, with context, exclusivity, and intention. Fans get what they've always wanted—real access to the artists they care about.

Our Story

This is the most exciting time to be an independent artist. You can create, distribute, market, and build a real career without waiting for permission from anyone. The tools are everywhere. The opportunity is real.

But there's been one piece missing. You release a song. It gets added to a playlist. People discover you at a show. They follow you on streaming platforms, maybe on socials. And then? They disappear into the feed. The algorithm moves on. You're left starting from scratch with every release.

We built Grouped because we lived it. We watched artists fill rooms, earn thousands of monthly listeners, generate real momentum—and still have no way to actually reach the fans they'd already earned. Every release felt like a reset. Every tour started cold.

So we asked a simple question: what if every fan you earned actually stayed? What if every release compounded on the last one? What if you could talk to your audience directly—no algorithm, no paywall, no noise?

That's Grouped. One place to capture who's listening, keep them close, and build something that grows with every release, every show, every moment. Not another tool. Not another dashboard. A growth engine built for lifelong music careers.

What We Believe

The principles behind everything we build.

◆

Own, don't rent.

Your fans belong to you, not an algorithm. Grouped gives you direct ownership of your audience—their emails, their data, their attention. No middlemen.

▴

Every release compounds.

Growth shouldn't reset with every drop. Your second release should build on the first. Your tenth should be unstoppable. That's how careers are made.

◯

Direct means direct.

When you want to reach your fans, you should be able to. No pay-to-play, no throttled reach, no guessing who sees what. Just you and your people.
