Setting Up Your Group

Everything you need to launch your artist-owned fan community.

What a Group Is

A Group is your artist-owned fan community space, built into the Grouped platform. It auto-populates with fans captured through Smart Swaps and other tools. No separate invites or onboarding friction required.

Read full article →
How to Launch Your Group
  1. Sign up for a Grouped account
  2. Set up your community name, header image, and welcome message
  3. Customize your welcome email
  4. Set up subscription tiers (free and/or paid)
  5. Connect Stripe for payments
  6. Share your Group link everywhere
Read full article →
Connecting Stripe

Stripe is required to accept payments on Grouped. Setup takes 2–3 minutes. You will need a bank account or debit card and either your SSN or EIN to complete the Stripe onboarding process.

ARTIST FAQs

Common Questions from Artists

Pricing, features, monetization, and data ownership.

How Much Does It Cost?

Grouped is free to use. There is no platform subscription fee. For revenue you generate, Grouped takes a 90/10 split after credit card processing fees — you keep 90%. A $0.30 platform fee is added on top of the fan's checkout price (passed to the fan, not deducted from the artist). Stripe handles payment processing at $0.30 + 2.9% per transaction.

Read more at Help Center →
What Features Are Currently Live?

Live features on Grouped include:

  • Smart Swaps
  • Groups (owned community space)
  • Livestreams
  • Email Lite
  • Music Player
  • Media Folders
  • Discussion Forums
  • Pay What You Want
  • Fan Voting / Polling
  • Free Trials
  • Subscription Tiers
  • Social Media Automations (via third-party tools)
Read more at Help Center →
Can I Monetize My Group?

Yes. Grouped supports four revenue streams:

  • Paid subscription tiers
  • Pay What You Want pricing
  • One-time media purchases
  • Tips

All payouts are processed through Stripe. You keep 90% after fees.

Read more at Help Center →
Who Owns the Fan Data?

You do. All fan emails, phone numbers, and engagement data belong to the artist. Grouped does not sell your fan data or use it for other purposes.

Smart Swaps

Smart Swaps

Smart Swaps is Grouped's fan capture tool. It creates a value exchange: fans get early access to music, and the artist gets their email, phone number, and pre-save. Smart Swaps achieves a 92% average opt-in rate. Share the link anywhere — bio, stories, ads — and every click becomes a fan capture point.
