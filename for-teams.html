FAQ

Questions from management teams

Can I manage multiple artists from one account?

Yes. Grouped's multi-artist dashboard lets you switch between artists instantly, view cross-roster analytics, and manage campaigns for your entire roster from a single login. No separate accounts needed.

How does pricing work for management teams?

Grouped uses credit-based pricing with no per-subscriber fees. You pay for what you send, not how many fans you have. Managing 10 artists with 100k total fans costs a fraction of what you'd pay across Mailchimp, Linktree, and other separate tools.

Can my artists access their own dashboards?

Yes. Grouped supports role-based access: admin, manager, artist (read-only), and collaborator. Artists can view their fan data and engagement metrics without modifying campaigns or settings.

What happens to fan data if an artist leaves our roster?

Fan data belongs to the artist. If an artist leaves your management, their data transfers with them. You retain aggregate insights and cross-roster analytics for your remaining artists.

Do you offer onboarding support for teams?

Yes. Management teams get dedicated onboarding support including account setup, data migration from existing tools, and training sessions for your team and artists.

How does Grouped replace Linktree, Mailchimp, and other tools?

Grouped is a direct-to-fan platform that replaces Linktree, Mailchimp, Toneden, Discord, and Chartmetric with one unified engine for artist management teams. Smart Swaps replaces link-in-bio, Custom Email replaces Mailchimp, Your Group replaces Discord, Fan Passes replaces SMS tools, Fan Profiling replaces analytics platforms, and Smart Feed replaces standalone websites.

Is there an API for custom integrations?

Yes. Grouped offers an API for custom integrations, allowing management teams to connect existing workflows, CRM systems, and reporting tools with the platform.

What kind of reporting can I get across my roster?

Cross-roster analytics include total fans captured, growth trends by artist, engagement metrics, campaign performance comparisons, and unified fan database insights — all from one dashboard.
